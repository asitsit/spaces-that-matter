Our founding partner

La Fabrique Spinoza

La Fabrique Spinoza is an independent think tank dedicated to citizen happiness. Pioneers in France on happiness at work, they have published many benchmark reports and led hundreds of conferences and workshops.

It was with La Fabrique Spinoza that Spaces That Matter co-created its scientific framework — drawing on more than 200 academic sources to define the 8 dimensions of the human performance of spaces.

Together, we share the conviction that measuring well-being is not an end in itself, but a concrete lever for action for organisations.
