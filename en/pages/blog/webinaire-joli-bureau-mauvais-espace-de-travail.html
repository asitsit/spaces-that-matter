Webinar: can a beautiful office be a bad workspace?

On Tuesday, December 8, 2026, from 12:00 to 12:45 pm (Paris time), Spaces That Matter is hosting an online session around an uncomfortable question: can a beautiful office be a bad workspace? The webinar is held in French.

Register on LinkedIn

Fine materials, plants, carefully chosen furniture, designed lighting: many companies have invested to make their offices more beautiful. That's good news. But does an office that looks great in photos make a place where people work well?

Why this question

A space can impress at first sight and disappoint day to day: hard to focus in, hard to meet colleagues in, hard to switch from a meeting to a call. Conversely, offices with little flair can support teams very well.

Looks matter, but they don't tell the whole story. What gives a workspace its value is what it does for the people who use it. That is exactly what Spaces That Matter measures, based on employees' experience and across 8 dimensions, from relational dynamics to the integration of nature.

"An office is judged less by how it looks than by what it makes possible for the people who work in it."

What we will cover

  • Why good looks alone don't make a good workspace.
  • What employees actually experience in their spaces, and how to measure it.
  • How to avoid design decisions that please at first sight but work against teams.

  • For: real estate, HR and workplace leaders, and everyone who designs workspaces
