01 · The dimensions

8 dimensions to measure it all.

Each dimension captures one facet of the lived experience of the workspace. Together, they form a complete dashboard of human performance.

Relational dynamics

This axis measures the ability of the space to encourage spontaneous, varied and fluid interactions between people, thereby stimulating collective dynamics.

Relationships · Collaboration · Conviviality

Emotional adaptability

This axis assesses the extent to which the work environment adapts to employees' emotional needs, allowing transitions between calm, stimulation and collaboration.

Well-being · Concentration · Flexibility

Spatial storytelling

This axis examines how spaces express the company's identity, values and culture in a visible and lived way.

Culture · Identity · Meaning

Engagement activation

This axis analyses how the space encourages active participation, initiative and cooperation between members of an organization.

Engagement · Attractiveness · Presence

Seamless hybrid work

This axis assesses how well places integrate hybrid working (on-site/remote), ensuring fairness, comfort and continuity of work.

Hybrid · Fairness · Digital

Openness to the outside

This axis assesses the extent to which spaces foster links with external stakeholders (clients, partners, local area), by cultivating hospitality, porosity and local roots.

Local area · Partnerships · Society

Spatial governance

This axis assesses how the layout of spaces influences people's inclusion and the quality of hierarchical relationships.

Participation · Co-creation · Evolution

Integration of nature

This axis assesses the extent to which the work environment fosters a sensory connection with the living world, through the presence of nature or sensory elements inspired by the natural world.

Biophilia · Nature · Light

02 · The process

From application
to label in 4 steps.

1

Subscription & roll-out

The organization subscribes to the STM offer. The questionnaire is then rolled out to all the employees concerned. Each person assesses their experience in their workspaces across the 8 dimensions.

Questionnaire time: ~15 min/employee Roll-out time: 2 to 4 weeks
2

Scoring & analysis

The responses are analyzed and summarized into an overall score and a score per dimension. The organization is positioned on each of the 8 axes and receives a detailed report.

Overall score Score per dimension
3

Debriefing (optional)

A working session with an STM expert to analyze the results in depth, understand the organization's specific dynamics and identify concrete action priorities.

2-hour session on site or by video call Personalized analysis
4

Award of the label

If the threshold is reached, the organization receives the STM label, valid for 2 years and renewable. You are certified — or you are not. If the label is not awarded, the report details the dimensions to strengthen as a priority.

Validity: 2 years Renewal encouraged with continuous improvement

04 · Compliance

A rigorous
and independent framework.

A label's credibility rests on the rigor of its assessment process and the independence of its assessors.

Scientific

Academic foundations

Our framework was developed with La Fabrique Spinoza, drawing on more than 200 academic publications in neuroscience, environmental psychology, ergonomics, organizational sociology and behavioral economics.

Independent

Peer assessment

Scores are calculated automatically from employees' responses. No external consultant is involved in the scoring — lived experience comes first.

Protected

Data & anonymity

Employee data is anonymized and processed in compliance with the GDPR. No individual response is accessible to the employer. Only aggregated results are shared.

Evolving

Continuous improvement

The STM framework is regularly updated to incorporate the latest research advances and changes in working practices. Each renewal benefits from the updated framework.
