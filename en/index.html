The observation

Today, companies invest heavily in their workspaces: renovations, innovative build-outs, new equipment — without always being able to measure their real impact on employees.

How can you tell whether a work environment truly improves team well-being, strengthens collaboration between departments, or fosters collective engagement day to day?

From this observation came Spaces that Matter, a tool that is both participatory and scientific, placing employees' lived experience at the heart of the performance of work environments.

The concept

8 dimensions to measure the human impact of your spaces

Our framework draws on neuroscience, environmental psychology, ergonomics, organizational sociology and behavioral economics — more than 200 scientific references.

Relational dynamics

This axis measures the ability of the space to encourage spontaneous, varied and fluid interactions between people, thereby stimulating collective dynamics.

Emotional adaptability

This axis assesses the extent to which the work environment adapts to employees' emotional needs, allowing transitions between calm, stimulation and collaboration.

Spatial storytelling

This axis examines how spaces express the company's identity, values and culture in a visible and lived way.

Engagement activation

This axis analyses how the space encourages active participation, initiative and cooperation between members of an organization.

Seamless hybrid work

This axis assesses how well places integrate hybrid working (on-site/remote), ensuring fairness, comfort and continuity of work.

Openness to the outside

This axis assesses the extent to which spaces foster links with external stakeholders (clients, partners, local area), by cultivating hospitality, porosity and local roots.

Spatial governance

This axis assesses how the layout of spaces influences people's inclusion and the quality of hierarchical relationships.

Integration of nature

This axis assesses the extent to which the work environment fosters a sensory connection with the living world, through the presence of nature or sensory elements inspired by the natural world.

The label

Spaces That Matter certification

The Spaces That Matter label is based on a questionnaire rigorously built with the Fabrique Spinoza, a think tank renowned for its work on the world of work. It draws on more than 200 scientific references.

1

Subscription & Roll-out

The company subscribes. The questionnaire is sent to all employees — they are the ones who assess their spaces.

2

Scoring & analysis

The responses are analyzed and produce an overall score across the 8 dimensions. A detailed report is delivered to the organization.

3

Debriefing (optional)

A session with our experts to analyze the results in detail and define concrete areas for improvement.

4

Award of the Label

The label is valid for 2 years and must be renewed, ensuring regular follow-up and continuous improvement of the spaces.
