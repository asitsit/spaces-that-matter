What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. It allows the website to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language and other settings, which can make your next visit easier and the website more useful.

Cookies used on this website

The website spacesthatmatter.com uses the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary technical cookies — essential for the website to function (session, display preferences). These cookies cannot be disabled without affecting how the website works. Duration: session or up to 12 months.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics / GTM) — measure the website's audience in aggregated and anonymised form (pages visited, session length, traffic source). This data is used solely to improve the user experience. Duration: up to 26 months.

Spaces That Matter does not use third-party advertising or profiling cookies.

Retention period

  • Session cookies: deleted when the browser is closed
  • Google Analytics cookies: 26 months maximum
  • Preference cookies: 12 months maximum

Managing your preferences

You can control and manage cookies at any time from your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome — Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari — Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect your browsing experience and limit some features of the website.

Google Analytics opt-out

To specifically disable Google Analytics tracking on all websites, you can install the official opt-out browser add-on provided by Google: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
