The Spaces That Matter team.

Strong, complementary expertise.

Laeticia Latil

Laeticia leads cultural and spatial transformation and brings her expertise in quality of working life and workplace surveys to a Spaces That Matter diagnosis centered on real lived experience and on building connections within spaces.

Maamoun Laariba

Maamoun designs and industrializes the data systems (collection, processing, dashboards) that make Spaces That Matter scoring reliable, robust and usable at scale.

Amélie Motte

Amélie brings in-depth expertise on the transformation of work (research, studies, frameworks) to anchor Spaces That Matter in a solid scientific foundation and a nuanced understanding of organizational challenges.

Alexandre Sitbon

Alexandre designs and runs participatory processes and the analysis of survey data to turn Spaces That Matter results into clear decisions and concrete action plans that teams take ownership of.
