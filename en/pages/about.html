Key facts

Spaces That Matter in a few facts

Spaces That Matter is the new label that measures the human performance of workspaces. Through a questionnaire completed by employees themselves, it assesses the real impact of offices on engagement, well-being, collaboration and performance.

Framework
8 dimensions, built with La Fabrique Spinoza on more than 200 scientific references
Questionnaire
Anonymous, about 10 minutes per employee
Participation
At least 50% of the employees concerned
Label validity
1 year, renewable
Data
Anonymized and processed in line with the GDPR
Founding team
Laeticia Latil, Maamoun Laariba, Amélie Motte and Alexandre Sitbon

Our story

Finally measuring the human impact of workspaces.

Companies invest in their offices with no tool to know what those spaces actually do for the people who use them. Building certifications measure technical performance; satisfaction surveys capture an opinion.

Spaces That Matter fills that gap. The framework was built with La Fabrique Spinoza, a think tank on well-being at work, on more than 200 scientific references.
